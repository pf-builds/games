# Peasant Swarm v2 — Build Spec (approved by the orchestrator 2026-09-24; Peter's direction is the contract)

Supersedes `SPEC.md` (v1, kept as the record) once v2 ships. Research behind every number: `claude-workspace/business/D-click-it-studios/game-research/peasant-swarm-v2/` (R1–R8, B0, C1, C2). Every number here is a starting value and lives in `config.json`. Units and ranges for every new key go in a `_units` block in config and are range-checked by `PS.selfTest`.

## 0. Rules for builders

- Vanilla JS, canvas 2D, no libraries, no build step. All art procedural in code, all audio synthesized. 100% owned.
- Every tunable in `config.json`. Bump `config.json?v=`, every `src/*.js?v=` and `style.css?v=` together with code (studio lessons 1, 11, 26, 30, 36).
- Sim time only for game-state delays (lesson 12). Bound every loop (lesson 7). No per-frame allocation on hot paths. Never `getImageData` on a cached canvas (lesson 27); verify caches by drawing into one scratch canvas (lesson 28).
- Keep `PS.selfTest`, `PS.fight`, `PS.simMatch`, `PS.step` working and extend them per milestone. `tools/harness.mjs` is the critic's tool; keep it green.
- Ideas outside the milestone go in `LATER.md`.

## 1. What v2 is

Same promise as v1: start as one peasant, recruit the valley, win clashes and the losers join you. Additions: terrain you route through (cliffs, canyons, a river with fords and bridges; passes are chokepoints), fog of war with five slightly slower rivals who play by the same fog rules, spoils on the map (relics that upgrade your whole swarm for the rest of the match, villages that join you, bandit camps that guard treasure), faster movement, better art. One correction from the data: v1 loses a match on one even clash (B0: 10 of 11 scripted losses were a single rout; the bell almost never rings), so v2 breaks fights locally and lets a beaten swarm keep a rear remnant.

Pillars: big is strong and big is loud; choose your fights in the dark; ground decides the fight; the flip is the payoff. The moment: a rival crowd comes out of the dark, you read its banner, pick your ground, and its peasants wash into your colour.

## 2. World and terrain

- **Grid.** World 4096×4096 px. Passability cell 32 px → 128×128. Art tile 64 world px (32 art px at 2×). Spatial hash 48 px, typed-array head/next lists (no per-tick array clears), radius-limited scans.
- **Layers**, flat typed arrays `c = y*N + x`: `terr` Uint8 (0 grass, 1 rock, 2 deep water, 3 ford, 4 bridge), `cost` Uint8 (255 blocked; 10 base, +6 within 1 cell of a wall, +2 within 2, +8 ford, +3 tree/rock cell), `sdf` Float32 (px to nearest blocked edge, cell centres, chamfer 3-4 minus half a cell), `region` Int16, `snap` Int32.
- **Generation**, seeded by `S.rng`, every step capped: six-fold macro template (playable disc r ≈ 0.47W, mountain rim in the corners, 6 spawns on a ring at 0.38W 60° apart, central meadow r ≈ 0.14W, a ridge or the river between each neighbouring spawn pair, each home meadow walled with exactly 2 exits so the opening is quiet); 3-octave value noise with ridged noise on ridge bands; threshold by quantile to `terrain.blockedFraction` (0.20); 3 cellular-automata passes; clear walls thinner than 2 cells; river rim to rim 2–3 cells wide bending away from home meadows with 4–5 crossings ≤ 1000 px apart (bridges 2 cells, full speed; fords 4–5 cells, `terrain.fordSpeed` 0.6); one pass 2–4 cells wide through every ridge plus 3–4 canyons into the centre (these passes are placed by the template so the path/straight detour lands in 1.25–1.45 by construction, not by reroll); flood-fill connectivity (fill pockets < 40 cells, carve a 2-cell corridor from any stray region to the main one); fairness (all spawns one region; nearest-rival path max/min ≤ 1.2; spawn-to-centre max/min ≤ 1.25; ≥ 2 exits per home meadow). Max 4 rerolls, then a baked fallback seed from `terrain.fallbackSeeds` (ship ≥ 8 vetted seeds in M1). Engine-safe: Fisher-Yates shuffles, no `Math.sin/cos/pow` in threshold tests. Target 15–30 ms desktop.
- **Placement.** Everything requires `region === main` and `sdf ≥ 2 cells`. Camps split across the 6 path-Voronoi regions. Fixed treasure at local maxima of path distance from the nearest spawn (dead ends). Timed power-ups where two spawns are about equally far. Nothing inside a pass. Trees and rocks: circular colliders bucketed per cell, never where `sdf < 3 cells`.
- **Seeds and replay.** `?seed=N`. Every sim random draw goes through `S.rng` (mulberry32): agent `atk`, `ph`, `face`, `thinkT`, attack jitter, damage jitter, neutral wander, trickle, chest slots, remnant pick. Render and particle draws stay on `Math.random`, and nothing gated on `onScreen`/`playerSees` may touch `S.rng`. Same seed + same input log = same `stateSig` (selfTest asserts it at 60 s).
- **Ground cache.** Static ground (grass with macro colour patches, terrain, pixel trails, decals) baked into 64 chunks of 512 world px (256 art px canvases, ~17 MB) with a one-cell apron; water chunks baked twice and swapped every ~500 ms. Camp dirt, props and their states are sprites, never baked. Every chunk passes an opaque-pixel check on a 4×4 downscale; on `visibilitychange` / `pageshow` / `contextrestored` rebuild visible chunks first, then one per frame. `PS.debugDropCaches()` blanks every cache so the harness can assert the rebuild.
- **Attract mode** reuses one map per title visit.

## 3. Time, movement, steering, camera

- **Fixed timestep.** Sim runs at exactly 1/60 s via an accumulator; live play catches up at most 2 sub-steps per frame and drops the rest; the hidden-tab fallback clock may run up to 8. Render interpolates nothing (accepted judder at 144 Hz). `frame()` clamps `raw ≥ 0`. All schedulers (field rebuilds, stamps, AI thinks) count ticks, not frames or wall time.
- **Speed.** `agent.speed` 170 px/s. Size curve: ×(1 + 0.15·max(0, 1 − n/40)) then −0.0005 per peasant above 40, floor 0.82. `steerLerp` 10, `arrive` 100, `joyLead` 340, huddle 0.70, speed power-up 1.30, hunt 1.07, flee 0.88, ford 0.6.
- **Terrain collision.** Bilinear-sample `sdf`; if under `r`, push out along the gradient once. Wall slide when `sdf < r + flow.slideMargin` (4 px): remove the velocity component pointing into the wall. Terrain push-out runs after every other position push. Skip both when the cell's `sdf > 2 cells`. Assert in debug: no agent ever inside rock or deep water.
- **Flow fields** (M2). One integration field per team, fast marching, 4-neighbour stencil, binary heap on typed arrays, early stop once every occupied cell plus a 3-cell margin is settled. Directions lazy per cell with a version stamp and a wall bias; agents take a bilinear blend of the 4 surrounding vectors. Rebuild when a team's target cell changes, at most one rebuild per tick, player first, player capped at 10 Hz. Route hysteresis: keep the old field unless the new route is ≥ 15% shorter at the centroid. The player's field runs on the player's knowledge grid (unexplored cells cost as grass) and re-plans when a revealed cell on the route is blocked; AI fields use true terrain (AIs know the land).
- **Boids blend.** Desired direction = field vector; inside the arrive radius (by path distance) switch to direct seek with arrive. Cohesion becomes (a) a speed multiplier `1 + k·(T_agent − T_teamMedian)`, k 0.004 per px, clamped 0.5–1.3, so leaders ease and stragglers hurry, and (b) local cohesion toward same-team neighbours found in the separation pass. Separation and the enemy hard push unchanged. `team.cx/cy` stays for the camera; `team.anchor` = centroid snapped to walkable for AI and labels.
- **Target snapping.** Cursor over rock: ray-cast from the centroid toward the cursor, last walkable cell. Joystick and keys: ray-cast along the input direction. `snap` only as fallback.
- **Camera.** `span = 520 + 24√n` world px; `zoom = clamp(shortSide/span, zoomMin, zoomMax)` quantised to the DPR step table (`camera.steps`: DPR 1 → 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5; DPR ≥ 2 → 0.5 to 1.5 in 0.25 steps; touch min 0.5, otherwise 0.75), 6% hysteresis, eased 1 s, re-quantised only at match start when adaptive DPR changes. Camera and shake round to device pixels. `camera.lerp` 6 with 0.35 s of velocity look-ahead capped at 22% of the half-extent, none under 40% speed. In a clash, centre between the two swarms.
- **Fixes from v1 (M1):** trickle checks `agents + campSize ≤ agentCap`; `agent.radius`, `flock.seek`, `ai.leadTime` wired or removed; `S.agents.filter` becomes swap-remove; every v2 field initialised in `mkAgent` (cloned shapes ran 6× slower in C2's test); recruitment reuses the neighbour gather; obstacle loop replaced by the bucketed check; AI hunt lead reads the prey's centroid velocity, not `prey.tx` (that is the player's cursor).

## 4. Population and supply

- Agent cap 1000 desktop, 700 touch (`spawn.agentCap`, `spawn.touchAgentCap`); bandits and garrisons count toward it; the harness runs both caps. Trickle cap 600 neutrals.
- Trickle every 2.5 s: 2 camps of 3–6, 80% biased across the two smallest living swarms, placed R+60 to R+360 px beyond that swarm's sight radius (found on the next screen, never seen popping in), never within `minTrickleDistFromTeams` of any other swarm, off during the finale.
- Per-swarm growth target on Normal (median scripted player): ~30 at 1:00, ~80 at 2:00, 120–180 at 3:00. All size gates in §8 are scaled to this curve.

## 5. Fog of war

- **Rule.** Terrain blocks movement, not sight. Three states: unexplored (blue-slate tint, `fog.unexploredAlpha` 0.92, slow cloud drift), explored (0.55, desaturated, terrain and camp dirt visible, no units, last-seen props), visible (clear; soft edge over the outer 28% of each source circle, ≥ 120 px band on phones).
- **Sight.** `fog.sight0 + fog.sightK·√n` = 340 + 10√n px around the team centroid, plus `fog.bucketSight` 200 px around every occupied 64 px bucket (a strung-out swarm sees along itself). Torches ×1.25 in the finale. AI uses the same rule scaled by difficulty senses (§7); no difficulty grants full vision.
- **Data.** Per team `vis` Uint16 stamped with the tick (visible when `vis[c] === tick`), `explored` Uint8, 32 px cells. Player stamps every 3rd tick; AI teams take turns, one per tick. Player display mask at 16 px with 8 padding cells so the world edge stays dark. Typed arrays are the truth; canvases are caches re-put on `visibilitychange` / `pageshow` / `contextrestored`.
- **Render.** One quarter-CSS-resolution fog canvas reused every frame: copy the mask subrect for the view (mask re-put only for the dirty rect, ≤ 5 Hz, 3×3 tent blur on the dirty rect), punch holes with `destination-out` from the player's stamp bucket list (not only the centroid) using one cached unit-space radial gradient, draw the vignette, the losing-clash red glow and the cloud drift into this same canvas, then one full-screen blit with smoothing on and back off. Exactly one full-screen alpha draw per frame for fog+vignette. Agents fade in/out over 120 ms via `seenA`. Hit flashes and HP bars respect `seenA`.
- **Leak audit.** Everything v1 gates on `onScreen()` gates on `playerSees()`: particles, hit and death sounds, smoke, rout ring, pickup bursts, team rings, name tags, minimap dots and power-ups, off-screen arrows, floaters, camp "+N" labels (explored ground shows the last-seen count, never live), prop states (village garrison, chest, bandit camp: last-seen), heavy-chest counters, the clash camera and name tags use only visible agents of a rival. Critic hooks: `PS.vis.sees(team, x, y)`, `?nofog=1`, a leak check that rivals drawn equals rivals visible. The harness bot reads `PS.vis`, never `PSS.teams` positions.
- **Tells through the dark.** Campfire smoke within `fog.smoke` 700 px; power-up beacon 650 px; clash noise within 1600 px (edge pitchfork icon, minimap ping, panned distant rumble, 3 s); rout ping folds into the clash ping; dust from swarms of 40+ within 1100 px (team-tinted minimap smudge and edge puff offset up to 120 px, no count); ghost "~N" marker for 12 s where a rival left sight; danger cue when an unseen rival is hunting you within 750 px (low horn, red edge chevron ±30°, at most every 10 s); crows flush above the fog when you come within 1.3× sight of a lurking Sly. Edge markers capped at 4, priority danger > visible rival > crown > ping > ghost.
- **Minimap.** Explored terrain only (parchment look here only), last-seen camps and props, rivals only while visible plus a 10 s fading ghost, pings, dust, crown. Redraw ≤ 15 Hz in one pass.
- **Rival pips.** Count only, live (the win condition must be readable; position stays secret), dimmed with "?" until that rival has been sighted once. At first sight a verdict mark (stronger / even / weaker, from count × power) shows on that rival's banner in the world for 2 s.
- **Arrows.** Solid arrow with count only for rivals visible but off-screen; hollow to ghosts; pitchfork to a heard clash; crown to the leader in the finale. Never an arrow to a rival you cannot see.

## 6. Combat and rout

- **Stats.** `agent.hp` 10, `agent.damage` 2 (still a 5-hit kill), `damageJitter` ±25%, attack interval 1.0 with jitter, engage radius 34, attack range 22. Attacking from fog adds nothing.
- **Local strength.** For each engaged pair (a, b): `L_a` = a's agents within `combat.localRadius` 150 px of the contact centroid (the mean position of a's and b's fighting agents), smoothed over `combat.moraleSmoothing` 0.3 s; `peak_a` = the max of `L_a` during this engagement. Whole-team totals never enter the break test. Side a breaks when engaged ≥ `engageDelay` 1.5 s (brace window) and (`L_a < breakRatio·L_b` held for `breakHold` 0.6 s, or `L_a/peak_a < moraleBreak` 0.6 and below b's ratio by 0.02). `minRoutSize` 3.
- **Rout group.** Only the loser's locally engaged group breaks: flood-fill from its fighting agents through same-team neighbours within 40 px. Of that group's survivors sorted by distance to the contact centroid: if the group has < `remnant.minLoser` 20 survivors, all flip; otherwise the nearest 65% flip (never any agent farther than `remnant.flipRadius` 200 px from the contact), the rest are the remnant. Agents outside the group keep their colour and keep fighting or fleeing as normal; a column strung through a pass loses its head, not its tail. In the finale (after the horn) routs are full flip, except that survivors routed by the crowned team scatter as neutrals (`finale.crownAbsorbs` false) so the leader cannot feed on attackers.
- **Remnant.** 6 s escape window (`remnant.escapeSeconds`): 1.3× speed, cannot attack and cannot be targeted (two-way), "hands up, run" frame, then 20 s REGROUP (AI: no hunting, head for camps away from the winner). A remnant cannot re-rout inside its window; a remnant of a remnant is just a smaller remnant. Banner: "+21 JOIN YOU · 9 FLED" (or "SCATTERED: 9 escaped" for the player). Player elimination only when count hits 0.
- **Spoils.** With several winners engaged with the loser, each flipping survivor joins the nearest winner's engaged agent (spoils by proximity, never by team index). Bandits never receive spoils and never rout.
- **Clash panel.** Count × power on both sides; verdict from that ratio and from local morale, so a 427 v 227 clash never opens as EVEN. "BULLY INCOMING" when a third swarm enters view within 400 px mid-fight.
- **Teams.** `S.teams` holds neutral(0), player(1), five rivals (2–6); arrays sized 9. Bandits live in `S.bandits` (camp objects with their own agents, team id 8 in agent records) and never enter `S.teams` loops; a `rivals()` iterator is the only way AI and HUD code walks teams.

## 7. Rivals

- **Five**, slots shuffled per match: Greedy (orange) racer, neutral bias 1.6, treasure 1.5; Bully (blue) hunter with scent; Wary (yellow) nomad, leaves any area with a clash heard within 800 px, prefers edges; Sly (crimson `#C8323C`) opportunist, goes to clashes heard within 1600 px, hunts a swarm that fought in the last 8 s at 0.9×, otherwise lurks at the pass nearest an open objective for ≤ 20 s (crows tell); Stubborn (violet `#9A62E0`) turtle, claims the nearest village or bandit camp site and holds 350 px around it, hunts at 0.8× inside its ring only, leaves at the horn. Player mint. Personality specials live in the personality objects in config. Hunt/flee ratios compare count × power. Neutrals tan.
- **Knowledge: fair on swarms, generous on landmarks.** Known from the start: terrain, passes, fords, objective sites (not their state). Camps: known within 700 px of spawn, then as explored or when smoke is in range; a camp seen empty is forgotten. Swarms: only inside the AI's own sight; leaving sight writes a ghost {x, y, vx, vy, count, t}: hunt 6 s projected 2 s ahead, flee 3 s, keep 20 s for routing. Noise at the player's radii. Two announced exceptions only: Bully's scent (player position ±250 px every 25 s after grace, Easy 40 / Hard 15; the first ping plays the horn and "BULLY HAS YOUR SCENT", later pings only a minimap pulse and a short low note) and the finale crown. Difficulty scales senses and tempo, never grants full vision or resources: sight ×0.9/1.0/1.15, memory 4/6/10 s, hearing 1200/1600/2000 px, think 1.1/0.9/0.5 s, `aiSpeed` 0.82/0.88/0.95, start bonus 5/3/0.
- **Rules that shape the match.** Grace `ai.grace` 60 s: no AI hunts, and AIs steer away from any swarm they see. Camp avoidance (always): skip a camp within 260 px of a visible rival at ≥ 0.7× own count (R8: the single biggest survival lever). AI-vs-AI hunt multiplier 2.2. Relax window 25 s after the player loses a fight (no rival starts a hunt on the player; Sly may still pile on after a win). Pile-on: once any swarm passes 1.6× the second, every rival that hears it fighting joins that fight ("THE VALLEY TURNS ON GREEDY"); spoils by proximity pay the pilers. Under the crown, a swarm under 0.3× the leader closes to 500 px and commits only when a second attacker is also within 500 px or it hears the leader fighting. Optional `combat.truceSeconds` (default 0): a visible "TRUCE 0:45" during which no attacks land and enemy swarms repel; enable only if the M4 harness still shows > 20% of eliminations inside the first minute.
- **State machine** per think: FLEE/cornered (Dijkstra flee target maximising threat distance minus own distance) → CROWN (finale) → HUNT seen or fresh ghost (10 s timeout, 12 s cooldown) → personality move (TRACK / INVESTIGATE / LURK / HOLD) → REGROUP (fresh remnant, 20 s) → FORAGE (value ÷ (path distance + 120) over known camps and objectives, never every agent; objective sight capped at 900 px) → EXPLORE (sample 12 unexplored cells 400–1000 px away, nearest away from threats, commit 3 s). Think 0.5–1.1 s, one rival per tick; assert in debug that no AI ever targets a swarm it has not seen or heard. Cost < 0.1 ms per tick.

## 8. Spoils (M6; scope may shrink after Peter's first playtest)

- **Three permanent axes**, team-wide, applied to every peasant including new recruits; the team's sprite atlas rebuilds on gain (one atlas per team, old canvases zeroed). Arms I–III: each tier +8% max HP and +8% attack speed (hat leather → iron → crested; tines iron → steel → gold). Boots I–II: +5% speed each and fords slow 40% less (dust puffs). Horn I–II: recruit radius 22 → 30 → 40 px (horn on the banner bearer). Power = harness-defined headcount equivalence, not a product: Arms II must fight evenly with ~1.35× base, Arms III with ≤ 1.7×; cut the per-tier percent if the harness says more. Arms III comes only from the red bandit camp or a routed leader.
- **Sources.** Earned: muster milestones at 15 / 40 / 90 neutrals recruited (absorbed rivals excluded) auto-grant Horn I, Boots I, Arms I. Found: typed relics (icon visible once the cell is explored) in 6 fixed chests at dead ends (one per spawn region), a trickle chest every 45 s after 1:00 with the underdog bias, heavy chests with a size gate (weight 15 → relic, 30 → pick 1 of 2; hold that many peasants in the ring 2 s, "12/30" counts up), bandit camps (green 5 bandits → Boots or Horn relic; orange 10 → Arms; red 20 at the centre → Arms, tier III allowed), and a routed leader drops its tiers as relics (45 s life). No gold, no shop, no prices, no towers (LATER).
- **Villages** ×6, one per spawn region, garrisons 8 / 10 / 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 held as numbers on the gate until they join: arrive at the gate ring with ≥ garrison and it joins at once; with fewer, hold the ring and it joins after `1.2 s × (garrison − your nearby count)`, 2 s minimum; a rival in the ring pauses the timer. Sprite 48×40 art px.
- **Bandit camps** ≥ 900 px by path from every spawn: hostile, 120 px leash, attack anyone within 90 px, never rout, never join, never receive spoils. Clearing opens the chest.
- **Rivals** value every objective with the same roam score and feasibility on count × power; they buy nothing because there is nothing to buy.
- **One objective grammar:** a ring with have/need for garrisons and heavy chests; timed power-ups stay glowing orbs with timer bars (Speed, Armor, Frenzy, Rally as v1, speed 1.30); permanent relics are scroll/chest pickups with a pip strip (3 axes) bottom-left above the timed buffs; banner + floater on every gain.
- **Records panel** (local, one localStorage key in try/catch): best peak, wins, fastest win per difficulty. No stat meta.

## 9. Match structure and finale

- `world.matchSeconds` 300 (6:00 stays in config for Peter to compare). 0:00–1:00 grace (recruit in the home valley, first glimpse around 0:35–0:65 with valleys). Scent from 1:00. Horn at 3:45 (`world.finalSeconds` 75): crown on the biggest swarm on every minimap, at the screen edge and in the world, position updated every 8 s then every 4 s in the last 20 s; "YOU ARE MARKED" for a leading player with a ghost crown where hunters think you are; torches ×1.25; `finale.crownAbsorbs` false; rivals commit in pairs. Dawn at the bell: fog lifts over 1 s before the result screen. Win: all rivals eliminated, or biggest at the bell. Lose: your count hits zero, or a rival is biggest at the bell.
- **Harness targets** (M8 tuning gates, measured over ≥ 20 seeded scripted matches with a fog-honest bot): median first sighting 30–90 s; first fight 60–150 s; ≥ 3 fights per match for the bot; ≥ 3.5 swarms alive at 3:00; bell reached in ≥ 60% of matches; the 3:45 leader wins ≤ 60% of AI-only matches; scripted win rate Easy ≥ 60%, Normal 35–45%; ≤ 20% of eliminations inside the first minute.

## 10. Controls and HUD

- **Touch: tap to go, drag to steer.** Tap (lift < 220 ms, moved < 12 px) routes through the player's field: tapping a visible rival chases its centroid, a camp/objective goes there, the minimap routes to that point; marker plus a dotted path preview through explored cells only, 1.5 s. Drag (> 12 px): v1 floating joystick, direct steering with wall slide, no pathfinding, cancels any route; ring 56 px, anchor clamped 72 px from every edge. Hold still (> 220 ms, < 12 px): stop and hold. Second finger: HUDDLE only. Lifting after a drag stops the swarm; a tapped route keeps running.
- **Desktop.** `input.desktopMode` "route": cursor-follow through the field with the 15% hysteresis; if the route is over 2× the straight line, draw it. Fallback mode "steer": cursor-follow is direct steering with slide and left-click routes. M2's critic tests route flip-flop with a cursor oscillating near an equal-cost line; if it fails the default flips to "steer". Minimap click routes there until the mouse moves on the canvas. Space huddle, WASD/arrows nudge, P pause, M mute, 1/2 pace.
- **Portrait 375×812** (safe insets all four sides): YOU chip top-left, timer centred, pause top-right (sound and pace move into the pause menu), rival pip strip under the top bar (five 60×24 pips), clash panel under that, relic strip + buffs bottom-left ≤ 96 px wide, HUDDLE 76×76 bottom-centre with an 88×88 hit area, minimap 116×116 bottom-right and tappable. Top HUD ≤ 130 px below the notch, bottom ≤ 140 px. **Landscape:** leaderboard top-left (YOU + 5 sorted, 20 px rows), minimap top-right 96×96, HUDDLE bottom-right 72×72, buffs bottom-left one row; nothing else in the right column. **Desktop:** v1 top bar with six chips and a 160 px minimap.
- **Onboarding without a text wall.** Two starter camps inside vision with "+N" labels and a ghost finger (touch) or cursor pulse (desktop) toward the nearest; the first muster relic icon flies into the strip; a bridge or ford before the next lure teaches routing; the first rival is seeded smaller than you within ~900 px and its pip flips from "?" to a count. First-ever match runs Easy silently with the difficulty picker on the end screen. Existing hint system carries the copy.
- **iOS/Safari.** `overscroll-behavior: none` on html and body; `touch-action: none` on the canvas, `manipulation` on buttons; preventDefault on `gesturestart` and `dblclick`; `pointercancel` treated as a lift; AudioContext resumed on first pointerup, on return to visible, and after "interrupted"; one vignette/fog canvas reused, resize debounced 100 ms, dropped canvases zeroed.

## 11. Art direction

Tidy Forager-lite: silhouette and outline carry information, palette small, terrain ≤ 45% saturation, team colours ≥ 70%.

- One `PAL` object (~36 colours in hue-shifted ramps: grass, stone, earth, water + foam, fog). One art pixel = 2 world px for everything. Camera quantised per §3.
- Team colour on the hat with a hat silhouette per team (hood, flat cap, wide brim, feathered cap, headband, pointed hood); neutrals in undyed tan with a straw hat; bandits in dark grey with a kerchief. Outline built by compositing (dark silhouette at 4 offsets under the sprite); v1's `outline()` readback is deleted. Hat pixels must be ≥ 25% of a crowd crop (R7 measured 12% in v1).
- Banner bearer per swarm at the centroid: pole twice peasant height, 3-frame pennant, emblem, growing at 10 / 50 / 150. The verdict mark and "+N join you" banner anchor to it.
- Ground chunks per §2 with macro colour patches from low-frequency noise, clustered decals, pixel trails, four camp dirt variants (as sprites).
- Cliffs by dual-grid autotiling (16 cases from the 32 px grid): lighter plateau top, one-cell south face in 3 strata with a lit lip and dark base, darker east/west rims, baked drop shadow south-east; mountains as 2-level plateaus with rocky tops and pines; canyon floors on the earth ramp with cracks and pebbles. Faces sit inside the blocked footprint; heads may overlap north lips (accepted; critics don't file it).
- Rivers: three water tones by shore distance, broken foam line, sparkle dashes, two baked frames per water chunk; fords as a sand bar with 4–6 stepping stones readable at zoom 0.5; bridges with plank deck, rails and a shadow strip.
- Hit sparks, contact-line dust, rout wave (survivors "drop fork, hands up" then flip in a wave from the contact line over ~0.6 s, render-only), HP bars hidden in swarms over ~20. Relic tiers as sprite overlays via the team atlas.
- Props at 2×: village 48×40, chest 12×10 with glint, heavy chest larger with the weight label, bandit tents, relic scrolls; pixel HUD icons replace emoji.
- Screens: code-painted valley title (4 parallax ridges, smoking village, bannered crowd, 2 fog strips; time-boxed), 1.2 s iris match start with rival pips as rumours, win = banner planted + jumping crowd + confetti, lose = banner falls + one cached desaturation pass + stats on a solid card. `?poster=1` stages a fixed scene for the arcade card. No `backdrop-filter` anywhere over the live canvas (B0: −35% fps).

## 12. Audio

Synthesized as v1 plus: war horn (finale; danger cue at lower pitch), scent horn and note, distant panned clash rumble (the one deliberate off-screen sound), chest and relic pickup, village cheer, remnant scatter, crown pulse, dawn chime, muster fanfare. Crowd murmur scales with your count. Everything else gated on `playerSees`.

## 13. Performance and QA

- **Budgets** (script time from `PS.bench`, never headless fps): named scene "cap clash" = 6 teams at the cap with two clashes on screen, fog on, 375×812 DPR 3 and 1280×720 DPR 1.
