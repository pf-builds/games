**Bake (final config):** 735 dailies (105 per weekday) + 500 practice, all distinct up to symmetry, each one re-parsed and replayed to a win at exactly par before writing. It took 57 s wall on 16 threads and is byte-identical across runs. daily.json is 206 KB, practice.json 139 KB. No below-band fallbacks were needed. Baked par spread: Mon 4:26 5:42 6:37, Tue 5:27 6:32 7:46, Wed 6:24 7:39 8:42, Thu 7:45 8:60, Fri 8:23 9:40 10:42, Sat 10:34 11:25 12:46, Sun 12:28 13:39 14:38.

**What the data says**
- Random layouts overshoot par. They don't undershoot it. Raw par medians run from 7 (Mon kit) to 17 (Sat kit), and the p90 reaches 32. The bands sit in the low tail of each distribution on purpose: par 14+ on a slide-everything board is past what most people will plan out. DeJam needed a hardening pass to raise par, and this game doesn't. Accept rates are only 1-11%, but the slowest day costs about 1.5 s of solve per accepted board, so supply isn't a constraint.
- 49-79% of random layouts are unsolvable (a pen whose doorstep is unreachable, the black sheep with no route). They're cheap to reject.
- The par line is close to unique: on 92-96% of its steps, exactly one direction keeps par. That suits a daily, because everyone's gold run is the same idea. The share text still carries no directions.
- Hard traps are rare on the par line. On average, less than one dead-end move is available along the whole solution, and 0.1-0.4 on swipe 1. Straying mostly costs extra swipes and doesn't lock the board, which fits unlimited undo plus medals.
- A free pen on swipe 1 is the norm (65-93% of in-band boards), so the Thu+ "no pen on swipe 1" rule does real filtering.
- The dead-end branch count mostly tracks the size of the search space (Mon ~12, Sat ~700). It works as a "not trivial" floor, not as a difficulty dial, and that's how it's used: the floors cut roughly the bottom 10-20% of in-band boards.
- The black sheep is the expensive element. One black sheep multiplies the states about 4-10x, because the colours can't be swapped. Two black sheep (Sun alt) hit the 250k state cap on 4% of layouts and push median par to 20. **Recommendation: at most 1 black sheep in v1.**
- Pens placed mostly inside the field (penEdge 0.2) made no real difference to par or accept rate, so 0.5 stays.

**Accept-criteria definitions (builder decisions, also in SPEC §3)**
- A dead-end branch is a move from a state that can still be won into a state that can't. Swipes that splash or do nothing are not moves.
- "Needs a stopper": every shortest solution includes a swipe in which a sliding sheep is stopped by another loose sheep. It's computed on the par-only move graph with those swipes removed. This is lenient on purpose, because it counts two sheep stacking against a fence. It still rejects 10-40% of in-band boards.
- "No pen on swipe 1": none of the four opening swipes pens a sheep.

**Rule-change proposal: none.** Every SPEC §1 rule shows up in the data doing distinct work. Mud raises median par by about 2 at the same size and sheep count. The pond takes directions away (Fri boards have fewer reachable states than Thu but higher par). Filled-pen-as-wall is what makes pen order matter. The one change I'd consider is a config limit, not a rule: cap black sheep at 1 (above).
