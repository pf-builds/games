- 2026-09-30 (M2 builder, the rules: mystery cards, linked squads, the locked space; notes in `tools/v4-m2-notes.md`). No rebake: `levels/levels.json` and `levels/teaching.json` are byte-identical, and a level with none of the twists plays exactly as before (`tools/regrade.js`, full, 0 differences on 75 levels). Written so the rules can be re-implemented from this entry alone; `tools/ref.js` is an independent implementation of it.
  - **Level format** (backward compatible; the header of `src/engine.js` documents it).
    - A card is `[mat, count]` or `[mat, count, flags]`. flags is 0 or 1; 1 = mystery. Anything else is an error.
    - `links: [[[col, i], [col, i]], ...]` pairs two cards by column (0-4) and index in that column as dealt (0 = the column's first card). The two must be in different columns, and a card is in at most one pair. A bad column or index is an error.
    - `lock: {key: [x, y]}`. The key must be a gilt cell (material 14) that is not a gate's key.
    - Warnings, not errors (`E.check(L, {linkRowGap})`): a mystery flag on a column's first card; partners dealt more than `v3.twists.linkRowGap` (2) rows apart; partners not in neighbouring columns (the rod would cross a column). The debug levels and the random injections in the tests keep to all three.
  - **Columns.** Each column is the list of the cards still in it, front first. A tap removes the front card. A pulled partner is removed from wherever it sits, and the cards behind it close up. Only a column's front card can be tapped.
  - **Mystery cards.** A mystery card is hidden while it is still in its column and is not the front. So a flag on a column's first card means nothing, a card that reaches the front is revealed for good (it only ever leaves its column by being played or pulled), and a partner pulled while hidden is revealed as it leaves (its squad shows in the line in its colour). The rules never read the flag. Event `REVEAL card column` when a mystery card becomes a front, or leaves hidden as a partner.
  - **Spaces.** Open spaces = the difficulty's spaces (`v3.rules[d].hold`) less the locked ones. Free = open less the spaces held. A squad always takes the lowest-numbered free space. While the lock holds, the locked spaces are the last ones, so every free space is below them.
  - **Linked tap.** A tap on a front card that has a partner is refused when fewer than 2 spaces are free: `REFUSED`, nothing in the state changes, exactly like v3.1's refusal. Otherwise, at the same engine instant:
    1. the tapped card leaves its column, then the partner leaves its column;
    2. the tapped card's squad takes the lowest free space, then the partner's squad the next lowest (events `TAP`, `TAP`, then `LINK space space`);
    3. it counts as one play;
    4. dispatch runs for every space in tap order (so the tapped squad before its partner), then the end checks.
    Either card of a pair can be the one tapped, whenever it is its column's front.
  - **Linked spaces.** Each squad of a pair dispatches for its own colour like any squad (SPEC-v3 §9's dispatch model). A squad is finished when no sapper of it is waiting and none is out. A Hard archer kill removes a sapper for good, so a killed sapper counts as finished. An unlinked squad's space frees when the squad is finished. A linked squad's space holds until its partner is finished too: when the second of the pair finishes, both spaces free at that instant, the earlier-placed first (two `FREE` events). The comparison flag `mergeLeftovers` (off) never merges into or out of a linked space.
  - **Locked space.** A level with a lock starts with `v3.twists.lockSpaces` (1) of its difficulty's spaces locked: Easy 6 → 5 + lock, Normal 5 → 4 + lock, Hard 4 → 3 + lock (never all of them: at most spaces − 1). The key is an ordinary gilt pixel, eaten by the Looters like any other. The moment it pops, the locked spaces open (event `UNLOCK cell`). A squad waiting for nothing but space can go at once: the next tap is taken while the Looters are still carrying the key home.
  - **Jam, generalized.** Checked once nothing is moving (no event pending), after the win check. If no card is left in any column: stuck (pixels left). Otherwise, if every front card's tap would be refused (unlinked: no free space; linked: fewer than 2), the level fails `jam`. `jamWhy` bit 1: a space was free (so a linked card needed 2); bit 2: a space was still locked. On a level without links this is exactly v3.1's jam. With it, every rest state is a win, a fail, or has a legal tap (the no-hang sweep in `tools/test.js`: 4,796 rest states on 41 twisted levels, none hung).
  - **Time.** Pairs, reveals and unlocks take no time; everything else is SPEC-v3's dispatch model.
  - **Dealing mode** (the dealer) never refuses. A pair (`playPair(m1, n1, m2, n2)`, or a linked tray card) with fewer than 2 open spaces fails `overflow`, like a squad with none. The lock counts there too.
  - **Grader** (`tools/grade.js`).
    - Every player picks only among legal taps: a front card whose tap would not be refused. On a level without links a patient player never meets a refusal, so every stored grade is unchanged. Stored winning orders never contain a refused tap.
    - The one-move lookahead reads the tray only through `grade.view(S)`: hidden cards show as colour 0 with their count and link; unseen sappers of colour m = the board's starting pixels of m less every card of m already seen (played, pulled or in view).
    - The only one-tap outcome that depends on a hidden colour is a linked front card whose partner is hidden. It is scored as the mean of its outcome with the partner played as each colour m with unseen[m] at least the partner's count, weighted by unseen[m] (fallback: every colour on the board, weight 1).
    - New: the fast tapper `grade.fast` (taps a random legal card the moment one exists, never waits for rest; `gapMs` between taps).
  - **The page.**
    - A hidden card's tile, in every row, is a neutral hatched tile (`layout.mystery`) with a gold "?" and its count. Its markup never holds its colour, crew or glyph, in colour-blind mode too. It turns over (`show.flipMs`) as it reaches the front.
    - Linked tiles are joined by a rod: an SVG over the tray, edge to edge when both are in the 3 visible rows. When the partner is lower down, a stub with two dots points toward its column. A refused linked tap shakes both tiles and toasts "Linked squads need 2 free spaces" (`layout.linkedText`).
    - Linked spaces wear a chain; a finished one holds its space, dimmed.
    - The lock's last space is a dashed socket with a padlock. The key block wears pulsing corner brackets in the socket's cream (`board.lockKey`); a gate's key keeps its full ring in the gate's tint, so the two read apart. On the unlock the `unlock` cue plays and the padlock pops (`show.unlockMs`).
    - The jam sheet says why: "Line jammed: linked squads need 2 free spaces, and … can't reach a block." or "… The locked space never opened." It names only stuck squads (on old levels, every squad at a jam).
  - **Debug levels.** `levels/debug-v4.json` (built by `tools/debug-v4.js`, `--check` rebuilds and diffs) is loaded only under `?debug=1`: a "v4 twists" row on the map and `SP.load(id)`, one-line hints, never in the save's progress. The four levels are copies of baked levels:
    - v4-mystery: e2-40 with every card behind a front flagged;
    - v4-linked: e1-20 with 3 links, the first starting as a stub;
    - v4-locked: e2-29 with a key one layer in;
    - v4-all: e2-38 with all three.
    The builder picks links that keep the one-move lookahead winning. Each has a verified winning order per difficulty, 13-16 taps.

  - **Rules text the functional critic found open (the game was already right; these sentences say what it does).**
    - **Wary.** A sapper is sent at a covered pixel only by a squad that is not yet wary. Covered: inside a standing tower's disc (below) and not itself a tower pixel; a tower stands while any of its pixels does. That sapper is an archer hit, its pixel is never claimed, and its squad turns wary at the same instant, the moment that sapper is sent (not when the arrow lands). From then on every sapper that squad sends goes for the nearest unclaimed reachable pixel of its colour outside every standing ring (none left: it waits). So a squad takes at most one hit. The flag belongs to the squad in its space and clears when a new squad takes the space.
    - **When the hit lands and when the sapper is home.** Let tiles = the pixel's walk distance + 1 (the step to its face, as for any sapper) and half = ceil(tiles / 2). The arrow lands at dispatch + yardMs + half × tileMs. Easy and Normal: the hit sapper is home again (back at its space, waiting to be sent) at hit + knockMs + yardMs + half × tileMs, i.e. its outbound half-walk mirrored at walking pace (tileMs, not carryMs), plus the knock-back. Hard (archersKill, and not the safeArchers level 51): it dies when the arrow lands.
    - **The disc.** A cell (x, y) (column, row) is inside tower t's disc when (x − cx)² + (y − cy)² ≤ r² (the edge counts as covered), where (cx, cy) is the mean of the (x, y) of the tower group's cells as the level loads (the 4-connected cells of the tower cell's material that belong to no other tower) and r is the level's `towers[t].r`.
    - **Short.** On every kill, colour m fails short when sappers(m) < pixels(m). sappers(m) = the total of every card of m in the deal (every column, hidden or not) less one for each pixel of m popped and one for each sapper of m killed: that is, every sapper of m in the tray, waiting at a space, or out and not yet spent (walking to a claimed pixel that hasn't popped, or on Easy and Normal a hit sapper walking back). pixels(m) = the pixels of m still standing (a claimed pixel counts until it pops). In dealing mode any kill fails.
    - **grade.ms.** The engine time of the patient replay's end: taps at the instants the engine goes quiet (no think time), and after the last tap the engine runs until nothing is pending. On a win that is the time the last squad is home after the winning pop, not the winning pop (e1-01: 22,200 ms against a last pop at 19,480). The time targets in the M3 entry are this number.

  - **Ring levels (the rules).** A level with `ring: true` is all picture inside a 1-cell ring of camp: every border cell (x = 0, y = 0, x = w − 1 or y = h − 1) is camp and no other cell is, else the level is malformed. Every rule of SPEC-v3 and this file holds with the ring as the camp:
    - connected ground is the walkable cells 4-joined to any ring cell;
    - a pixel's distance is the smallest BFS distance (over connected ground, every ring cell at 0) of a walkable 4-neighbour of it; tiles = distance + 1; timing, dispatch, claims, spaces, refusal, jam and win unchanged.
    - **The tie-break** (only between pixels at equal distance; distance always comes first). For cell (x, y) on a w × h board: layer = min(x, y, w − 1 − x, h − 1 − y); let a = layer, x1 = w − 1 − a, y1 = h − 1 − a. Its side and place on that layer, tested in this order: if y = a and x < x1, side 0 (top), pos = x − a; else if x = x1 and y < y1, side 1 (right), pos = y − a; else if y = y1 and x > a, side 2 (bottom), pos = x1 − x; else if x = a and y > a, side 3 (left), pos = y1 − y; else (only the centre of an odd square) side 0, pos 0.   - **Power-ups are engine operations** (`power(k, a, t)`; `canPower(k, a)` answers the same question without acting). Like a tap, the clock first runs to t (if given); then the power-up is taken or refused. They take no engine time, are not plays (the play count and every card's tap number are unchanged) and are logged as `POWER k a` (plus the `REVEAL` and `FREE` events below). k: 0 Ladder, 1 Quartermaster, 2 Scout, 3 Recall (`E.PW`).
    - **Not playing** (won, failed) or **dealing mode**: nothing happens (`NOPLAY`).
    - **Refused** (`REFUSED`): nothing in the state changes and nothing is counted (byte-identical, as v3.1's refused tap). A power-up is refused when the level has used `rules.powers[k]` of it already (config `meta.powers[k].perLevel`: Ladder 1, Quartermaster 3, Scout 1, Recall 2; rules without `powers`, as the grader's and the dealer's, allow none), or when its own test below fails.
    - After a power-up is taken the end checks run as after a tap (win, and at rest stuck and jam); by the tests below none of them can end the level.
  - **Ladder** (k 0; a ignored). Taken when the line has fewer than 8 spaces (MAXLINE). The line gains one space for the rest of this attempt (Retry resets it). Locked spaces stay the last ones: with the lock still shut, the new space is open and sits just below the locked one, which moves up a number. `POWER 0 s`, s = the new open space (the open count less one).
  - **Quartermaster** (k 1; a = a card, by its index in the file: column order, front first). Taken when the card is still in its column (not played, not a pulled partner), is not the front, and has at most `rules.pullDepth` (config `meta.pullDepth`, 2) cards still in the column ahead of it; and, **only when nothing is moving (no event pending)**, when after the pull some front card's tap would be taken (the pulled card as its column's front, every other column's front as it is). Then the card moves to the front of its column and every card it passes steps back one place, in order. A hidden mystery card is revealed (`REVEAL card column`). A linked card stays linked (its tap still pulls its partner). `POWER 1 card`.
  - **Scout** (k 2; a ignored). Taken when at least one card is hidden. Every hidden card is revealed (`REVEAL card column` each, in file order). `POWER 2 n`, n = how many.
  - **Recall** (k 3; a = a space). Taken when the space holds a squad that came from a card, is not linked, has sappers waiting and none out (every sapper of it is at its space; on Easy and Normal an archer-hit sapper walking back counts as out). Then that card goes back to the **front of the column it was tapped from** (ahead of the column's current front, if any), its count now the number of sappers that were waiting (the rest were spent on pixels or, on Hard, killed); the space frees at once (`POWER 3 space`, then `FREE space mat`).
