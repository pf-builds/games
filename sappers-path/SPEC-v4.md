## 9. Decisions log (builders append)
- 2026-09-30 (M1 builder, the look pass; notes in `tools/v4-m1-notes.md`, palette report in `tools/v4-m1-palette.md`). Presentation only: no rule changes, no rebake, `levels/levels.json` byte-identical.
  - **Flat studs.** Every material pixel is a solid face with rounded corners on a 1 CSS px seam of its own colour darkened (half the seam from each neighbour), a soft highlight along the face's top, a faint foot and a small glint (`board.stud`). No mark inside. The v3 fix pass's per-block tones (lit, shade, bond) are set to 0 (`board.tones`), so every block of a colour is the same stud. Checked readable at 8 CSS px a cell (320×500, `tools/shots-v4-m1/320x500-l64-8px.png`).
  - **Markers that always show:** the iron gate keeps its bars and the gilt key its key glyph, in both modes; towers keep their crenellated rim and archer; gates keep the padlock and keys the tinted ring. Ground, water and scenery are unchanged (dark and muted).
  - **Colour-blind mode** (off by default) brings back v3's per-material marks on every block and a small material glyph on the queue tiles. The toggle sits on the title (beside Era map) and the map's top bar, and is kept in the v3 save (`settings.cb`, strict boolean on load). The glyph is the block's mark used as a CSS mask, so it takes the tile's text colour.
  - **Palette retune.** New colours for all 14 materials, picked by a seeded search in OKLCH with each material kept in its hue family and lightness band. Every pair of materials that stand together in a level is now ΔE00 25.5 or more (was 12.3; 64 levels had a pair under 20, now none). Pairs 10+ apart in lightness: 71 of 87 (was 65). Gilt (keys) is a dark old gold, 25.5+ from anything it stands with. `tools/palette.js` measures and searches.
  - **Colour-first queue.** Each tile is a solid block of its material's colour with a big count: no crew name, no swatch (names stay in the coach text, fail messages and the tiles' aria-labels). `layout.queueRows` = 3 visible rows, 5 columns: the front row is the raised button you tap; the two rows behind are the same size, flat and faded (opacity .66, then .38), so three moves ahead read at a glance. A row past a column's end is hidden but keeps its space, so the layout never jumps. Blocked (lock badge) and safe marks from v3.1 are unchanged. SPEC-v3 §3's "the next 3 cards are visible behind it" becomes the next 2.
  - **Holding spaces** are bigger and bolder (50 px tall on a phone, 36 px count): an empty space is a dark socket, a taken one a solid tile in its colour. Stuck (hatch and lock, the count outlined dark so it stays readable) and working (gold rim, walking marker, stepping figures) and the "3 stuck · 1 working · 1 free" head are unchanged.
  - **Phone order** stays header (map, level, retry, speed, sound), board, holding line, queue. The rail's sizes are CSS variables (`--tile-h`, `--slot-h` and friends) set per layout: phone, short phone (max-height 700), small iframe (max-height 640), wide side panel, short landscape. No power-up bar and no dead buttons (M5).
  - **Fit fix.** A level's line and queue now render before the board is fitted, so the board gets the room that is really left (before, the 8 unhidden line slots made the rail two rows tall at fit time and cost the board about 2 CSS px a cell on a phone).
  - **Speed.** The top bar's speed button cycles `show.speeds` [1, 2, 3] and shows its multiplier (gold above 1×). The save keeps `settings.speed` (a whole number 1–3; an old save's `fast: true` loads as 2). The victory march plays at the faster of the button's speed and `show.victoryPace` (1.5). `show.speedFast` is gone.
