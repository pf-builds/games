## 3. Real pace (`tools/grade.js` pace) and its calibration

**What it measures.** The stored Normal winning order is replayed. Each next card is tapped the moment its tap is legal (a space free, the tap not refused). The result is that time × `duration.pace.factor`.

**If the early taps lose the level,** the patient time stands in and the bake logs it. This happened 0 times in the Siege and 0 times in the Gallery.

**Calibration on the v4.1 levels 26-100 (b26e4a4):**
- The raw replay takes a median 42.3 s (10th to 90th percentile 29.8-59.2 s, max 82.4 s).
- Patient play takes a median 150 s.

Peter reported 60-90 s at 1× and 20-30 s at 3×.

**The factor 1.77 fits both reports:** 42.3 × 1.77 = 75 s at 1×, and 75 / 3 = 25 s at 3×, each the middle of his range. His range maps to factors 1.42-2.13.

**A wall-clock think time per tap cannot fit both.**
- About 2.85 s per tap gives 75 s at 1×.
- But at 3× the think times that fit his 1× range give 53-85 s, not 20-30 s.
- So his rhythm follows the game (he waits on what the sappers do), not his own thinking.

**An engine-time wait fits both too.** Waiting about 2.9 s of game time per tap gives 75 s at 1× and 25 s at 3×.

I used the factor because the brief defines the replay as tapping the moment a space is free.

**The two models disagree on the new boards, by about a third.** Measured on the final levels 26+, the engine-time wait model gives a median of 157 s, against the factor's 231 s (0.61-0.76 of it, median 0.66). Under the wait model a level's length follows its tap count (at most 55) more than its walking. A few timed v4.2 playthroughs would settle which one is Peter (LATER).

**Targets** (bake config `duration.pace`):
- From level 26: real pace in 180-300 s (the boss 180-420 s), aiming at a median of 240 s.
- Levels 1-25 keep v4.1's patient limits, unchanged and not rebaked.
- The patient max no longer applies from 26. Patient times are still reported, and `regrade.js` checks the stored real pace.
- The Gallery takes the same targets with a 150 s floor: its pictures have no moats, so they walk shorter.

## 4. Squads and the dealer

- **Squad sizes:** cards are dealt at 90-99 sappers (the Gallery at 45-99). A squad that would run a tap past the 15 s dead-time cap shrinks by 0.85 per step, up to 16 steps.
- **Bigger squads first:** with `deal.best` 3 (new, opt-in: 1 deals exactly as before), each squad is the biggest of the first three colours that fit. There are 48 deal attempts a candidate.
- **The 15 s dead-time cap is what binds.** From the bottom entry, the top of a 42 × 41 board is about 80 tiles away, which is 13.6 s of walking for a single round trip. So squads that must go far stay small, and every generated level's longest tap sits at 14.5-15.0 s.
