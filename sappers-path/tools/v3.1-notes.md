Then: "the primary failure mode is having all five slots full with colors that cannot clear", and the victory march at 1.5x. The decisions are logged in SPEC-v3 §9.

## Rules
- `play()` returns `E.REFUSED` (-3) when no space is free (and the card can't merge). The check comes after the clock runs to `t` and before the column head moves, so the state stays byte-identical. `E.NOPLAY` (-2) is the old "not playable".
- `tapFails` is renamed `blocked(m)`. `stuck(s)` is new: a space whose squad is all home and has nothing it may go for (a wary squad: nothing outside the rings).
- `nomove` is renamed `jam`. The overflow fail is gone from play; `tap()` keeps it only for dealing mode (`gen.js` rejects a deal whose squad finds no space).
- `tools/ref.js` does the same, written separately: refuse before consuming the card, `jam` at rest.

## Proof that levels don't change
- `tools/regrade.js`: every level on every difficulty, bake seeds (`L.seed`; teaching `seedOf(n, 0)`) and bake playout counts: random-tap rate (400), stored winning line (won, peak, taps, engine ms), Normal lookahead player (100). 525 checks: **0 differences** against `levels.json`. The same script on the pre-change engine: also 0, so the stored grades were current.
- Why: a patient tap never meets a full line (a full line at rest was already `nomove`), and nothing else changed.

## Page
- Refused tap: the card shakes (Web Animations, `show.blockedShake`, `blockedShakeMs`) on every tap; the toast `layout.blockedText` and cue `blocked` at most once per `show.blockedGapMs`. `playCol` returns false. No lock.
- Line: `readLine()` fills `app.li` (stuck, working, free, near, full, danger) with no allocation. Slot classes `stuck` (hatch and lock), `work` (gold rim, marker, stepping figures). Head texts: `lineText`, `nearText` "One space left", `fullText` "Line full: wait for a squad to come home", `marchText`; counts use `stuckWord` / `workWord` / `freeWord`. The `.slot.last` pulse now only shows in the near-jam state; the `warn` cue plays on the tap that makes it.
- Cards: `.card.blocked` (dimmed, lock badge) replaces `.card.fatal`.
- Jam: the `jam` cue (the old overflow recipe) when it's found; the sheet names up to `layout.jamNames` crews.
- Victory march: `marchCheck()` after a tap that empties the tray: a scratch sim loads the state and runs `quiet()`. A win sets `app.march`; `paceNow()` = `pace × max(2x if on, victoryPace)`.
- Coach: level 2 step 3 "If every space is stuck waiting, you lose."
- Cache: `?v=15` everywhere, including the font URL in style.css (a stale `?v=14` there was the only console warning).
