Written by `tools/bake.js` (the Siege, between the bake markers) and `tools/gallery-bake.js` (the Gallery, between the gallery markers); the text outside the markers is by hand. Notes: `tools/v4.2-notes.md`. Decisions: `SPEC-v4.md` §9, the v4.2 entry. Bake config v11, Gallery bake v3.

The tables below are the fix-up run's (they cover all 100 levels); the main run's log follows them. Level 99 is the one variety soft pick (46.4% alike its era's earlier picks; the era median holds). Real pace = the stored Normal order replayed tapping the moment a space is free, x 1.77 (calibration in the notes). Levels 1-25 keep v4.1's grades and have no real-pace figure.
