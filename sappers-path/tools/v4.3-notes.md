# Sapper's Path v4.3: notes

Peter's five calls (plan section "v4.3", 2026-10-03): a holding space frees at its squad's last pickup; linked squads go
only when both cards are at a front; one fixed tag per level, no difficulty picker; the Gallery opens one picture at a
time; progress saved by level id. All 160 levels rebaked under the new rules and tags. The exact rules are in SPEC-v4 §9
(the v4.3 entry); the per-level table is `tools/v4.3-rebake.md`.

## 1. The rules (engine.js, ref.js)

- **Freeing at pickup.** A squad holds its space while any of its sappers is waiting at it, walking out to a pixel, or
  (Easy, Normal, or a safeArchers level) hit by an arrow and walking back. A sapper whose block popped is a carrier: it
  walks home and belongs to no space. A Hard kill is done. So the space frees at the pop of the squad's last block (or the
  kill that ends it); a linked pair frees when both squads are done, the earlier-placed space first. `spO` now counts the
  sappers walking out or back; `S.out` still counts everyone away, carriers included.
- **The level ends when everyone is home.** Won at the last pop, as before; rest (the jam and stuck checks, the patient
  player's next tap, the real-pace clock, `grade.ms`) waits for nothing moving, carriers included. The page's sheet waits
  for that too: `show.settleCapMs` is gone.
- **Linked squads at the front.** A linked card is legal only when its partner is the front card of its own column
  (`S.why(j)` 3 otherwise, checked before the two spaces, why 2). A refused tap changes nothing. At rest, a linked front
  waiting for a buried partner counts as refused for the jam; `jamWhy` bit 4 says so.
- **Quartermaster** may pull a linked card or a partner forward. Its at-rest jam check now judges the columns as they
  would be after the pull (fixed during v4.3: the engine looked at the columns before the pull, so a pull that passed
  another linked front's partner jammed the line at once; ref.js had it right; a test covers it).
- **Recall's "none out"** means none walking out or back (carriers aren't the squad's).

## 3. Dealing and grading on the tag (bake.js, gallery-bake.js, gen.js)

- One stored order per level (`win[tag]`) and one grade (`grade[tag]`): rate, lookahead, fast tapper, dead time, patient
  time and real pace, all on the tag's rules. Tuning targets the band on the tag's rules too.
- `--boards FILE` keeps every level's board (grid, palette, gates, towers, lock) and deals it again; only a level whose kept
  board deals nothing draws new forts (`newBoards` in the bake record).
- Dealing stays on Hard's rules (4 spaces, archers lethal), so every deal wins on any tag.
- **Links.** `gen.deck` places a pair's partner at the end of a neighbouring column at the moment the pair is played, so
  every column is a subsequence of the play: in the stored order both cards of each pair are fronts when it is tapped, and
  no two pairs can be cross-buried (pair P ahead of Q in one column and Q ahead of P in another). tools/test.js checks
  both on every baked and debug level.
- Teaching levels keep their shipped boards and decks (`teach-v4.js --boards`: each still passes every check on its tag
  under the new rules; only its tag and order change). The coach for 62 and 77 now says the new linked rule; 77's lock
  step went (with its partner now in front of the Looters the lock opens before the step could show).
- Debug levels play on Normal, orders re-solved.

## 4. The time target (real pace)

The target stayed v4.2's: real-pace median about 3:45 (225 s) with the ×1.77 model (about 3:00 for Peter), ranges 180-300 s
(the boss 180-420 s) from level 26, the Gallery 150-300 s; patient limits before 26 (4-15 at 60-90 s, the rest 240 s).

- **What the new rule does to the same levels.** v4.2's levels replayed under the v4.3 rules on their tags: the Siege 26+
  went from a median of 226 s to 171 s; the Gallery from 197 s to 169 s. A space that frees at the pickup lets the next
  squad go a walk home sooner, and the line was the bottleneck.
- **Deals barely move it on a kept board** (untuned, default deal: era 2 boards 122-169 s; changing squad sizes 90-99,
  45-70, 25-45: medians 171-184 s). Fresh forts of the same era are no slower (era 2 median 155 s).
- **What moved it: letting squads wait.** From Era 2 the dealer takes the first colour that fits instead of the biggest of
  three (`best` 1, v4.2 had 3) and lets up to 3 squads wait at rest (`park` 3; Era 2 also deals a squad that can't reach
  yet first 30% of the time, `deep` 0.3). More squads stand on the line waiting for their colour, so fewer colours dig at
  once. Untuned medians: era 2 231 s, eras 3-4 208 s. The picker then aims the real pace at 225 s among candidates that
  meet every target (`duration.pace.aim`, `aimWeight` 5 s per point of rate).
- **The Gallery:** squads of 30-60 instead of 45-99 (more taps; each squad holds its space a shorter while), untuned
  median 195 s; aim 200 s (its v4.2 median 197 s).
- Bigger boards or a slower walk were not used: boards keep v4.2's sizes, the walk is Peter's (LATER).

## 5. Hard levels with archers

- **The finding.** Every colour is dealt exactly as many sappers as it has blocks, so on Hard (archers kill) one kill leaves
  its colour short and the level fails. v4.2's deals were made patient (tap, wait until nothing moves). Replayed at real
  pace (a tap the moment a space frees) on Hard, 47 of v4.2's 50 Era 3-4 levels lost, most within the first 20 taps; with
  1, 2 or 4 s of thinking after each tap most still lost (8 sampled: 1 won at 1-2 s); waiting 8-15 s won, and playing
  patiently while the archers stand takes 344-774 s of model time. As forced tags, those Hard levels would have been lost by anyone playing at a normal rhythm.
- **Rushed dealing** (`gen.js` `D.rush`, `deal.rushHard`; on a Hard level whose archers stand, not on 51): a second
  simulation plays each squad the moment a space frees beside the patient one, and a squad is kept only if both survive
  (the rushed one also run to rest with no more taps). linkUp and the tuner's resizes check the whole play rushed
  (`gen.rushLine`). The bake requires the real-pace replay to win on the tag (a lost replay counts as a duration miss).
- **Result:** all 12 Hard levels with archers (55, 60, 64, 69, 73, 75, 78, 82, 87, 91, 96, 100) win at real pace and
  patiently. The same order replayed with 1, 2 and 4 s of thinking (`grade[tag].thinks`, reported, preferred on ties) wins
  all three on 10 of them; 82 loses at 2 s and 100 at 1 and 2 s. In-between rhythms can still lose on those two (LATER).
- Rushed dealing failed on two kept boards (91 and 100: every candidate's deal failed within its attempts), so those two
  levels have new boards at the same size (`newBoards`), the only boards that changed in the Siege.

## 6. Saves by level id (save.js, meta.js)

- Format 2: `{v: 2, done: {id: 1}, gal: {id: 1}, settings: {muted, speed, cb}, last, coins, inv, best: {id: [ms, taps,
  coins]}, lives}`. A cleared id the page has is kept wherever it sits; openness comes from the ids (`isOpen`: the first,
  a cleared level, or the one after a cleared level; the Gallery's first picture opens on its gate).
- Migration from format 1 (written by every shipped version, v3 to v4.2; same key `sappers-path.v3`): any difficulty bit
  makes a level cleared; a best row keeps the fastest time and the fewest taps over the difficulties it was won on, and its
  coins; coins, inventory, lives and the other settings are kept; `diff` (and v3's `fast`, read as speed 2) dropped.
- `tools/saves/{v3,v4,v4.1,v4.2}.json` are example saves written by each shipped version's own save.js and meta.js
  (`tools/saves/make.js` takes them from git and plays a scripted player: mixed difficulties, a Gallery won out of order,
  a power-up bought and used). tools/test.js migrates each and reads it back unchanged; the harness migrates a junk
  format-1 save in the page and checks the next write is format 2.
- Coins: 5/10/20 a win by tag, +10/20/40 on a level's first clear (was: on a new medal).

## 10. The critics' tools: checks that no longer apply

Not updated here (the brief: the critics update their own tools). What each one would now report, and why:

- **All three rule diffs** (`tools/critic-v4-1/diff.mjs`, `tools/critic-v4.1/diff.mjs`, `tools/critic-v4-2/diff.mjs`, each
  with its own `rules.mjs`):
  - Their space frees when every sapper of the squad is sent and home. v4.3 frees it at the last pickup, so every `FREE`
    event comes earlier, and every tap after it (rushed games) lands on a different line: event-by-event comparisons,
    times, statuses and `jamWhy` differ on essentially every level and game. Their rules need the v4.3 freeing rule (SPEC
    entry, first bullet) before any comparison means anything.
  - Their linked tap pulls a buried partner out of its column. v4.3 refuses it (`why` 3). Every linked level (the debug
    levels and the Siege's linked levels) differs from the first such tap, and their refused-tap counts differ.
  - `jamWhy` bit 4 (a linked front waiting for a buried partner) is unknown to them.
  - They loop over Easy, Normal and Hard reading `L.win[d]` and `L.grade[d]`. The level files now hold one order and one
    grade per level, on its tag (`win[L.tag]`, `grade[L.tag]`); the other difficulties' orders are gone, so their stored-order
    replays find nothing (or crash) on two of the three. Random patient and rushed games on all three rule sets still make
    sense as a rules comparison.
  - `critic-v4.1/diff.mjs`'s dealer park check (informational): from Era 2 the dealer now lets up to 3 squads wait at rest
    (`dealBy.era<e>.park`), so its limit of 2 would flag levels that are within the new limit.
- **The edge cases** (`critic-v4-1/edge.mjs`: stuck, `jamWhy` 3, a linked reveal on pull, after-fail status; `critic-v4-2/
  edge.mjs` and `critic-v4.1/edge.mjs`: the Quartermaster's at-rest jam test, Recall of a wary squad, Ladder with the lock
  shut, Scout after a Quartermaster):
  - "Linked reveal on pull" by a tap no longer happens: a partner must already be at a front (face up). A hidden partner
    is revealed only by a Quartermaster pull or by reaching its column's front.
  - The Quartermaster's at-rest jam test must judge the columns after the pull (v4.3, the engine fixed to match ref.js).
  - Recall's "none out" now ignores carriers; their wary-squad case keeps its outcome but its timings move with the freeing.
  - `critic-v4-1/edge.mjs`'s quiet-vs-stepped check over every stored order reads `L.win[d]` for all three difficulties.
- **`critic-v4.2/pace.mjs`** recomputes the real pace from `L.win.normal` on Normal's rules and compares `grade.normal.pace`.
  v4.3 measures it on each level's tag (`win[tag]`, `grade[tag].pace`, the tag's rules: 4 spaces and lethal archers on Hard),
  with the v4.3 freeing rule, so its rules need both changes and its fields need the tag.
- **The `play.mjs` page drivers** call `SP.load(id, diff)` and `SP.winOrder(d)`: the difficulty argument is ignored now
  (every level plays on its tag) and `winOrder()` returns the level's one order. Any of their checks that expect three
  difficulties per level, a difficulty picker, medals on the report, or every Gallery picture open at once no longer apply.
